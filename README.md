# 🚚 Supply Chain & Logistics Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard offers a comprehensive evaluation of supply chain performance and logistics operations. It focuses on analyzing shipping dynamics, carrier efficiency, transit durations, and cost distribution across various transportation modes to optimize operational efficiency and revenue management.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Business Intelligence:** Power BI (DAX, Data Modeling, Custom Visuals)
- **Data Source:** Supply Chain Dataset (SKUs, Transportation Modes, Carriers, Routes, Logistics Costs)

## 📊 Key KPIs & Insights
- **Average Shipping Time:** **5.75 days** across all active shipping routes.
- **Total Shipping Costs:** Generated over **$554.81K** in total shipping expenses.
- **Total Cost:** Overall logistics costs reached **$52.92K**.
- **Transportation Modes:** Evaluated shipping cost per unit across Road, Rail, Air, and Sea modalities.
- **Geographic Insights:** Analyzed shipping costs per unit and transit durations across key locations (Bangalore, Chennai, Delhi).
